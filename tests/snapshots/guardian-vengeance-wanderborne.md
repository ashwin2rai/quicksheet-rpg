## Features

### Ancestry: Human

**High Stamina.** Gain an additional Stress slot at character creation.

**Adaptability.** When you fail a roll that utilized one of your Experiences, you can **mark a Stress** to reroll.

### Community: Wanderborne

**Nomadic Pack.** Add a ***Nomadic Pack*** to your inventory. Once per session, you can **spend a Hope** to reach into this pack and pull out a mundane item that's useful to your situation. Work with the GM to figure out what item you take out.

### Class: Guardian

**Unstoppable.** *(your Unstoppable Die: d4)* Once per long rest, you can become *Unstoppable*. You gain an Unstoppable Die. At level 1, your Unstoppable Die is a **d4**. Place it on your character sheet in the space provided, starting with the 1 value facing up. After you make a damage roll that deals 1 or more Hit Points to a target, increase the Unstoppable Die value by one. When the die's value would exceed its maximum value or when the scene ends, remove the die and drop out of *Unstoppable*. At level 5, your Unstoppable Die increases to a **d6**.

While *Unstoppable*, you gain the following benefits:

- You reduce the severity of physical damage by one threshold (Severe to Major, Major to Minor, Minor to None).
- You add the current value of the Unstoppable Die to your damage roll.
- You can't be *Restrained* or *Vulnerable*.

**Frontline Tank.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to clear 2 Armor Slots.

### Subclass: Vengeance (Foundation)

**At Ease.** Gain an additional Stress slot.

**Revenge.** When an adversary within Melee range succeeds on an attack against you, you can **mark 2 Stress** to force the attacker to mark a Hit Point.

### I Am Your Shield

*Valor 1 - Ability - Recall 1*

When an ally within Very Close range would take damage, you can **mark a Stress** to stand in the way and make yourself the target of the attack instead. When you take damage from this attack, you can mark any number of Armor Slots.

## Character Hook

**Appearance:** Dresses in royal clothes, has the air of a wrestler, and is recognizable by a voice that carries farther than expected.  
**Personality:** Quick to forgive and slow to forget.  
**Motivation:** Wants to keep a promise made to a dying mentor.  
**Backstory:** Corin Hallowmere left a mining settlement deep in the hills the season her mentor disappeared on an ordinary errand, and has spent every day since trying to keep a promise made to a dying mentor.
