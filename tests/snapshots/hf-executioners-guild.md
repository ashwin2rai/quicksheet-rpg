## Features

### Ancestry: Ribbet

**Amphibious.** You can breathe and move naturally underwater.

**Long Tongue.** You can use your long tongue to grab onto things within Close range. **Mark a Stress** to use your tongue as a Finesse Close weapon that deals **d12** physical damage using your Proficiency.

### Community: Freeborne

**Unbound.** Once per session when you roll with Fear, you can change it into a roll with Hope instead.

### Class: Assassin

**Marked for Death.** *(Marked for Death damage: +1d6, equal to your tier; d6s from Ambush)* On a successful weapon attack, you can **mark a Stress** to make the target *Marked for Death*. When you deal damage to a target you've *Marked for Death*, add a number of **d4s** equal to your tier to the damage roll.

You can have only one adversary *Marked for Death* at a time. This condition lasts until you take a rest, the current adversary *Marked for Death* is defeated, or the GM spends a number of Fear equal to your tier to clear it.

**Get In & Get Out.** **Spend a Hope** to ask the GM for a quick or inconspicuous way into or out of a place you can see. The next roll you make that acts on this information has advantage.

**Deadly Determination.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to clear 2 Stress.

### Subclass: Executioners Guild (Foundation)

**First Strike.** The first time in a scene you succeed on an attack, you deal double damage.

**Ambush.** Your "Marked for Death" feature uses **d6s** instead of **d4s**.

### Whirlwind

When you make a successful attack against a target within Very Close range, you can **spend a Hope** to use the attack against all other targets within Very Close range. All additional adversaries you succeed against with this ability take half damage.

## Character Hook

**High Concept:** Shadow Agent Looking for a Home  
**Trouble:** Sticky Fingers  
**Appearance:** Dresses in sinister clothes, has the air of a butcher, and is recognizable by an elaborate braid with a single bead for every year on the road.  
**Personality:** Dry-humored and quietly observant.  
**Backstory:** Gus Foxglove grew up in a sleepy farming village. Everything changed when a beloved teacher was arrested on a false charge. Now Gus wants to prove that a long-held family story is true.
