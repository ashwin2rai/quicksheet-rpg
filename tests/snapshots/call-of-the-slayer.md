## Features

### Ancestry: Faun

**Caprine Leap.** You can leap anywhere within Close range as though you were using normal movement, allowing you to vault obstacles, jump across gaps, or scale barriers with ease.

**Kick.** When you succeed on an attack against a target within Melee range, you can **mark a Stress** to kick yourself off them, dealing an extra **2d6** damage and knocking back either yourself or the target to Very Close range.

### Community: Highborne

**Privilege.** You have advantage on rolls to consort with nobles, negotiate prices, or leverage your reputation to get what you want.

### Class: Warrior

**Attack of Opportunity.** If an adversary within Melee range attempts to leave that range, make a reaction roll using a trait of your choice against their Difficulty. Choose one effect on a success, or two if you critically succeed:

- They can't move from where they are.
- You deal damage to them equal to your primary weapon's damage.
- You move with them.

**Combat Training.** You ignore burden when equipping weapons. When you deal physical damage, you gain a bonus to your damage roll equal to your level.

**No Mercy.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to gain a +1 bonus to your attack rolls until your next rest.

### Subclass: Call of the Slayer (Foundation)

**Slayer.** *(Slayer Dice (maximum stored): 1d6, equal to your Proficiency)* You gain a pool of dice called Slayer Dice. On a roll with Hope, you can place a **d6** on this card instead of gaining a Hope, adding the die to the pool. You can store a number of Slayer Dice equal to your Proficiency. When you make an attack roll or damage roll, you can spend any number of these Slayer Dice, rolling them and adding their result to the roll. At the end of each session, clear any unspent Slayer Dice on this card and gain a Hope per die cleared.

## Character Hook

**High Concept:** Axe-for-Hire and Amateur Astronomer  
**Trouble:** My Rescuer Expects Repayment  
**Appearance:** Dresses in sparing clothes, has the air of a gladiator, and is recognizable by a patch over one eye that is sometimes switched to the other.  
**Personality:** Superstitious about everything except real danger.  
**Backstory:** Greer Calloway left a ruined castle repurposed as a school the season a rival family burned the family business to the ground, and has spent every day since trying to return a stranger's lost letter to its intended reader.

**Background question:** Who taught you to fight, and why did they stay behind when you left home?
