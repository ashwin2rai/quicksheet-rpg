## Features

### Ancestry: Drakona

**Scales.** Your scales act as natural protection. When you would take Severe damage, you can **mark a Stress** to mark 1 fewer Hit Points.

**Elemental Breath.** *(Breath element: electricity)* Choose an element for your breath (such as electricity, fire, or ice). You can use this breath against a target or group of targets within Very Close range, treating it as an Instinct weapon that deals **d8** magic damage using your Proficiency.

### Community: Orderborne

**Dedicated.** *(Your sayings: "Waste nothing."; "Respect is earned in deeds, not words."; "Truth spoken softly still carries.")* Record three sayings or values your upbringing instilled in you. Once per rest, when you describe how you're embodying one of these principles through your current action, you can roll a **d20** as your Hope Die.

### Class: Ranger

**Ranger's Focus.** **Spend a Hope** and make an attack against a target. On a success, deal your attack's normal damage and temporarily make the attack's target your *Focus*. Until this feature ends or you make a different creature your *Focus*, you gain the following benefits against your *Focus*:

- You know precisely what direction they are in.
- When you deal damage to them, they must mark a Stress.
- When you fail an attack against them, you can end your "Ranger's Focus" feature to reroll your Duality Dice.

**Hold Them Off.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** when you succeed on an attack with a weapon to use that same roll against two additional adversaries within range of the attack.

### Subclass: Wayfinder (Foundation)

**Ruthless Predator.** When you make a damage roll, you can **mark a Stress** to gain a +1 bonus to your Proficiency. Additionally, when you deal Severe damage to an adversary, they must mark a Stress.

**Path Forward.** When you're traveling to a place you've previously visited or you carry an object that has been at the location before, you can identify the shortest, most direct path to your destination.

### Nature's Tongue

*Sage 1 - Ability - Recall 0*

You can speak the language of the natural world. When you want to speak to the plants and animals around you, make an **Instinct Roll (12)**. On a success, they'll give you the information they know. On a roll with Fear, their knowledge might be limited or come at a cost.

Additionally, before you make a **Spellcast Roll** while within a natural environment, you can **spend a Hope** to gain a +2 bonus to the roll.

### Untouchable

*Bone 1 - Ability - Recall 1*

Gain a bonus to your Evasion equal to half your Agility.

## Character Hook

**Appearance:** Dresses in tactical clothes, has the air of a child, and is recognizable by a habit of humming quietly.  
**Personality:** Soft-spoken until something matters.  
**Motivation:** Wants to find a place that truly feels like home.  
**Backstory:** Ines Millbrook left a mining settlement deep in the hills the season she won a contest that was meant to be lost, and has spent every day since trying to find a place that truly feels like home.

**Background question:** Your first kill almost killed you, too. What was it, and what part of you was never the same after that event?
