## Features

### Ancestry: Earthkin

**Stoneskin.** Gain a permanent +1 bonus to your Armor Score and damage thresholds at character creation.

### Community: Highborne

**Privilege.** You have advantage on rolls to consort with nobles, negotiate prices, or leverage your reputation to get what you want.

### Transformation: Shapeshifter

**Change Shape.** During a rest, you can use a downtime move to swap your current ancestry with another. When you do, describe how your appearance changes.

**Only Skin Deep.** *(Benefits only from the ancestry's first feature, listed above)* You gain the benefit of only one of your chosen ancestry's features, which you select when you choose the ancestry. You can use a downtime move to choose a different feature from that ancestry.

### Class: Brawler

**I Am the Weapon.** Your barehanded attacks are as strong as any blade. You have a primary weapon called Brawler's Strike equipped while you have no other Active Weapons. It uses a trait of your choice, has Melee range, and deals **d8+d6** physical damage using your Proficiency (both the d8 and d6 scale off your Proficiency). While this weapon is active, you gain a +1 bonus to your Evasion.

**Combo Strike.** *(Combo Die: d4)* After rolling damage on a successful attack with a Melee weapon, you can **mark a Stress** to start a combo strike. When you do, roll your Combo Die and note the result, then continue rolling your Combo Die until the result of your latest roll is lower than the roll that preceded it. You deal extra damage equal to the total of all rolled Combo Die results on this attack. The results can't be modified by any means.

Your Combo Die starts as a **d4**. Once per tier, you can increase your Combo Die by one step as a level advancement option.

**Square Up.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to intimidate a target within Close range, making them temporarily *Vulnerable*.

### Subclass: Juggernaut (Foundation)

**Rugged.** Gain a permanent +3 bonus to your Severe damage threshold.

**Overwhelm.** When you succeed on an attack against a target, you can **spend a Hope** to throw the target within Close range or to force them to mark a Stress.

## Character Hook

**High Concept:** Dreamy Monk  
**Trouble:** The Duke's Daughter Fancies Me  
**Appearance:** Dresses in haphazard clothes, has the air of a golden retriever, and is recognizable by a voice that carries farther than expected.  
**Personality:** Grimly practical, with a hidden soft spot for strays.  
**Backstory:** The story in a crumbling hilltop monastery is that Ursel Underhill left after he won a fortune and lost it the same night. The truth is stranger, but the goal is simple: Ursel means to learn the truth about where he came from.

**Background question:** Who did you recently lose a fight to that you're desperate for a rematch against?

**Transformation question:** Someone you loved dearly abandoned you when they found out about your ability to change shape. Who was it, and what happened?

**Connection prompts** (ask another player):

- What is one thing we're both afraid of?
- I still haven't forgiven you for something you said to me. What was it, and why did you say it?
