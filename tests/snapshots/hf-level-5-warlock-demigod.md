## Features

### Ancestry: Simiah

**Natural Climber.** You have advantage on Agility Rolls that involve balancing and climbing.

**Nimble.** Gain a permanent +1 bonus to your Evasion at character creation.

### Community: Reborne

**Found Family.** Once per rest, you can **spend a Hope** to use an ally's Experience as if it were your own. When you do so, describe how your time with that ally prepared you for this moment.

When you join a new community or rediscover your old one, you can permanently trade this card for that one instead.

### Transformation: Demigod

**Gifted.** You gain a +1 bonus to action, reaction, and damage rolls.

**Weight of Divinity.** When you fail a roll, you must mark a Stress or the GM gains a Fear.

### Class: Warlock

**Patron's Pact.** *(Patron: the Glass Leviathan, sphere of influence Artists; Patron Die: d8)* You have committed yourself to a supernatural entity—such as a god, fae, or demon—in exchange for power. Write their name on your character sheet, then work with your GM to determine their sphere of influence (such as Nature, Chaos, Wisdom, Mischief, Love, War, Justice, or Death). Before making an action roll that relates to your patron's sphere of influence, you can **spend a Favor** to call upon their aid, rolling your Patron Die and adding its result to the total. Your Patron Die starts at a **d6** and increases to a **d8** at level 5.

**Favor.** *(Favor: 3, starting Favor; you can hold at most 6)* You start with 3 Favor. You can use a downtime move to show tribute to your patron. Describe how and gain Favor equal to your Spellcast trait. Additionally, when you succeed on an action roll with Hope, you can choose to gain a Favor instead of a Hope.

*Note:* The maximum Favor you can hold at one time is 6.

**Patron's Boon.** *(Hope feature)* When you fail a roll, you can **Spend 3 Hope** to reroll with advantage.

### Subclass: Pact of the Endless (Foundation)

**Patron's Mantle.** **Spend a Favor** to cloak yourself in a terrifying aspect of your Patron that lasts until you take Severe damage or the scene ends. While this effect is active, you gain a bonus to your damage thresholds equal to your tier and have advantage on action rolls to intimidate a target.

**Deathless Embrace.** Once per rest, **spend any number of Favor** to roll an equal number of Patron Dice. For each result of 4 or higher, clear a Hit Point.

### Subclass: Pact of the Endless (Specialization)

**Harrowing Invocation.** When an adversary targets you or an ally within Very Close range with an attack, you can **spend a Favor** to give them disadvantage on the roll. If the adversary fails the roll, they must also mark a Stress.

**Damage Sink.** Once per rest, you can **spend a Favor** to halve incoming damage.

### Tell No Lies

*Grace 2 - Spell - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 2.*

Make a **Spellcast Roll** against a target within Very Close range. On a success, they can't lie to you while they remain within Close range, but they are not compelled to speak. If you ask them a question and they refuse to answer, they must mark a Stress and the effect ends. The target is typically unaware this spell has been cast on them until it causes them to utter the truth.

### Umbral Veil

*Dread 1 - Spell - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 3.*

Once per rest, you can **mark a Stress** to encase yourself in shadowy energy. When you do, place a number of tokens on this card equal to the number of Fear in the GM's pool. After an attack roll is made against you, you can spend any number of tokens to give the result a -1 penalty per token spent.

At the end of the scene, clear all unspent tokens.

### Hypnotic Shimmer

*Grace 3 - Spell - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 3.*

Make a **Spellcast Roll** against all adversaries in front of you within Close range. Once per rest on a success, create an illusion of flashing colors and lights that temporarily *Stuns* targets you succeed against and forces them to mark a Stress. While *Stunned*, they can't use reactions and can't take any other actions until they clear this condition.

### Summon Horror

*Dread 4 - Spell - Recall 2*  
*Acquired at level 4.*

Once per scene, **mark a Stress** to summon an otherworldly creature that deals **d8+1** magic damage using your Spellcast trait to a target within Far range. If the target marks any Hit Points from this attack, they must succeed on a **Reaction Roll (12)** to steel themselves against the horror or mark an equal number of Stress.

After making the attack, the creature dissipates.

### Siphon Essence

*Dread 2 - Spell - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 5.*

Make a **Spellcast Roll** against a target within Very Close range. Once per long rest on a success, the target takes **d12+4** magic damage using your Proficiency. On a success with Fear, you gain a +1 bonus to your Proficiency for this attack.

You clear a number of Hit Points equal to the number of Hit Points the target marked from this attack.

### Voice of Dread

*Dread 1 - Spell - Recall 0*  
*Acquired at level 1.*

You can magically speak to a creature you can see, tormenting them with your words. Make a **Spellcast Roll** against them. On a success, they must mark a Stress and are frozen with terror, making them temporarily *Restrained*.

### Enrapture

*Grace 1 - Spell - Recall 0*  
*Acquired at level 1.*

Make a **Spellcast Roll** against a target within Close range. On a success, they become temporarily *Enraptured*. While *Enraptured*, a target's attention is fixed on you, narrowing their field of view and drowning out any sound but your voice. Once per rest on a success, you can **mark a Stress** to force the *Enraptured* target to mark a Stress as well.

## Character Hook

**High Concept:** Aging Demon Dealer  
**Trouble:** Sleepwalks Into Danger  
**Appearance:** Dresses in lavish clothes, has the air of a soothsayer, and is recognizable by a limp that worsens before rain.  
**Personality:** A rule-breaker slowly learning why the rules exist.  
**Backstory:** Few in a library city where the streets are named after books expected much of Kestrel Starling, until someone mistook them for a person of importance and they played along. Since then, Kestrel has set out to laugh in the face of the creature that haunts their nightmares.

**Background question:** Your patron has given you one task you must accomplish above all else. What is it, and why does it worry you?

**Transformation question:** Who bestowed demigod status upon you, and what trial did you complete to earn this gift?

**Connection prompts** (ask another player):

- I once did something very foolish, and you've never let me live it down. What was it?
