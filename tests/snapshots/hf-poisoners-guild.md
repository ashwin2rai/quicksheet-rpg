## Features

### Ancestry: Goblin

**Surefooted.** You ignore disadvantage on Agility Rolls.

**Danger Sense.** Once per rest, mark a Stress to force an adversary to reroll an attack against you or an ally within Very Close range.

### Community: Warborne

**Brave Face.** Once per session when you would be forced to mark a Stress, you can **spend a Hope** instead.

### Class: Assassin

**Marked for Death.** *(Marked for Death damage: +1d4, equal to your tier)* On a successful weapon attack, you can **mark a Stress** to make the target *Marked for Death*. When you deal damage to a target you've *Marked for Death*, add a number of **d4s** equal to your tier to the damage roll.

You can have only one adversary *Marked for Death* at a time. This condition lasts until you take a rest, the current adversary *Marked for Death* is defeated, or the GM spends a number of Fear equal to your tier to clear it.

**Get In & Get Out.** **Spend a Hope** to ask the GM for a quick or inconspicuous way into or out of a place you can see. The next roll you make that acts on this information has advantage.

**Deadly Determination.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to clear 2 Stress.

### Subclass: Poisoners Guild (Foundation)

**Toxic Concoctions.** **Mark a Stress** to place **1d4+1** tokens on this card. When you make a successful weapon attack, you can spend a token to afflict the target with a poison. You know these poisons:

- ***Ghost Petal:*** The target becomes temporarily *Vulnerable*.
- ***Grave Spore:*** The target must also mark a Stress.
- ***Leech Weed:*** You deal an extra **1d6** damage on this attack.

When you take a long rest, clear all unspent tokens.

## Character Hook

**High Concept:** Royal Taster Turned Poisoner, The Sandeford Who Got Away  
**Trouble:** Pride Before Every Fall  
**Appearance:** Dresses in padded for silence clothes, has the air of a judge, and is recognizable by a tiny bell sewn to the hem of a sleeve.  
**Personality:** Grimly practical, with a hidden soft spot for strays.  
**Backstory:** The story in a glassblowers' quarter is that Isolde Sandeford left after he alone walked out of a collapsed mine. The truth is stranger, but the goal is simple: Isolde means to earn enough to rebuild a ruined family home.

**Background question:** You once killed someone you were close to. What happened, and how did it change you?

**Connection prompts** (ask another player):

- What secret about myself did I tell you, and how did it change your view of me?
- I've killed someone for you. Who were they?
