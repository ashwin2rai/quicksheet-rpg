## Features

### Ancestry: Fungril

**Fungril Network.** Make an **Instinct Roll (12)** to use your mycelial array to speak with others of your ancestry. On a success, you can communicate across any distance.

**Death Connection.** While touching a corpse that died recently, you can **mark a Stress** to extract one memory from the corpse related to a specific emotion or sensation of your choice.

### Community: Warborne

**Brave Face.** Once per session when you would be forced to mark a Stress, you can **spend a Hope** instead.

### Class: Brawler

**I Am the Weapon.** Your barehanded attacks are as strong as any blade. You have a primary weapon called Brawler's Strike equipped while you have no other Active Weapons. It uses a trait of your choice, has Melee range, and deals **d8+d6** physical damage using your Proficiency (both the d8 and d6 scale off your Proficiency). While this weapon is active, you gain a +1 bonus to your Evasion.

**Combo Strike.** *(Combo Die: d8, raised 2 steps by level advancements)* After rolling damage on a successful attack with a Melee weapon, you can **mark a Stress** to start a combo strike. When you do, roll your Combo Die and note the result, then continue rolling your Combo Die until the result of your latest roll is lower than the roll that preceded it. You deal extra damage equal to the total of all rolled Combo Die results on this attack. The results can't be modified by any means.

Your Combo Die starts as a **d4**. Once per tier, you can increase your Combo Die by one step as a level advancement option.

**Square Up.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to intimidate a target within Close range, making them temporarily *Vulnerable*.

### Subclass: Martial Artist (Foundation)

**Stance Fighter.** *(Known stances are listed under Martial Stances; Focus: 6, the most you can hold)* You can channel your inner resolve to shift into martial stances that grant you special benefits in combat. Take the Martial Stances sheet and choose two martial stances from Tier 1. Each time you level up your character, choose an additional stance from your tier or lower.

### Martial Stances

Focus: once per rest during a moment of calm, you can clear your Focus track, then roll a number of d6s equal to your Instinct and gain Focus equal to the highest result rolled. You can hold a maximum of 6 Focus. You can spend a Focus to shift into a martial stance you know; you can't have more than one active stance at a time. You drop out of your active stance when you shift into a different stance, take Severe damage or mark your last Hit Point; otherwise, at the end of the scene.

- **Quick** (Tier 1): When you make an attack, you can spend a Focus or mark a Stress to target another creature within range with that attack.
- **Reliable** (Tier 1): Gain a +1 bonus to your attack rolls.
- **Defensive** (Tier 2; learned at level 2): Attack rolls targeting you from within Melee range have disadvantage unless the attacker marks a Stress to negate the disadvantage.
- **Aggressive** (Tier 2; learned at level 3): Gain a −1 penalty to your Evasion. On a successful attack, roll an additional damage die and discard the lowest result.
- **Anchored** (Tier 2; learned at level 4): Gain a +2 bonus to your damage thresholds. While in this stance, you can't be moved against your will.
- **Grappling** (Tier 3; learned at level 5): On a successful attack within Melee range, you can spend a Focus or mark a Stress to temporarily *Restrain* the target or throw the target up to Close range.
- **Vigilant** (Tier 3; learned at level 6): When you are targeted by an attack, you can mark a Stress to gain a d6 bonus to your Evasion against the attack.

### Ferocity

*Bone 2 - Ability - Recall 2*  
*Acquired at level 2.*

When you cause an adversary to mark 1 or more Hit Points, you can **spend 2 Hope** to increase your Evasion by the number of Hit Points they marked. This bonus lasts until after the next attack made against you.

### Body Basher

*Valor 2 - Ability - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 3.*

You use the full force of your body in a fight. On a successful attack using a weapon with a Melee range, gain a bonus to your damage roll equal to your Strength.

### I Am Your Shield

*Valor 1 - Ability - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 4.*

When an ally within Very Close range would take damage, you can **mark a Stress** to stand in the way and make yourself the target of the attack instead. When you take damage from this attack, you can mark any number of Armor Slots.

### Goad Them On

*Valor 4 - Ability - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 5.*

Describe how you taunt a target within Close range, then make a Presence Roll against them. On a success, the target must mark a Stress, and the next time the GM spotlights them, they must target you with an attack, which they make with disadvantage.

### Inevitable

*Valor 6 - Ability - Recall 1*  
*Acquired at level 6.*

When you fail an action roll, your next action roll has advantage.
