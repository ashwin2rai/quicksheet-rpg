## Features

### Ancestry: Drakona

**Scales.** Your scales act as natural protection. When you would take Severe damage, you can **mark a Stress** to mark 1 fewer Hit Points.

**Elemental Breath.** *(Breath element: electricity)* Choose an element for your breath (such as electricity, fire, or ice). You can use this breath against a target or group of targets within Very Close range, treating it as an Instinct weapon that deals **d8** magic damage using your Proficiency.

### Community: Orderborne

**Dedicated.** *(Your sayings: "Truth spoken softly still carries."; "Teach what you have learned."; "Owe nothing you cannot repay.")* Record three sayings or values your upbringing instilled in you. Once per rest, when you describe how you're embodying one of these principles through your current action, you can roll a **d20** as your Hope Die.

### Class: Sorcerer

**Arcane Sense.** You can sense the presence of magical people and objects within Close range.

**Minor Illusion.** Make a **Spellcast Roll (10)**. On a success, you create a minor visual illusion no larger than yourself within Close range. This illusion is convincing to anyone at Close range or farther.

**Channel Raw Power.** Once per long rest, you can place a domain card from your loadout into your vault and choose to either:

- Gain Hope equal to the level of the card.
- Enhance a spell that deals damage, gaining a bonus to your damage roll equal to twice the level of the card.

**Volatile Magic.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to reroll any number of your damage dice on an attack that deals magic damage.

### Subclass: Elemental Origin (Foundation)

**Elementalist.** *(Element: air)* Choose one of the following elements at character creation: air, earth, fire, lightning, water. You can shape this element into harmless effects.

Additionally, **spend a Hope** and describe how your control over this element helps an action roll you're about to make, then either gain a +2 bonus to the roll or a +3 bonus to the roll's damage.

### Rain of Blades

*Midnight 1 - Spell - Recall 1*

**Spend a Hope** to make a **Spellcast Roll** and conjure throwing blades that strike out at all targets within Very Close range. Targets you succeed against take **d8+2** magic damage using your Proficiency.

If a target you hit is *Vulnerable*, they take an extra **1d8** damage.

### Wall Walk

*Arcana 1 - Spell - Recall 1*

**Spend a Hope** to allow a creature you can touch to climb on walls and ceilings as easily as walking on the ground. This lasts until the end of the scene or you cast Wall Walk again.

## Character Hook

**Appearance:** Dresses in flamboyant clothes, has the air of a wolf in sheep's clothing, and is recognizable by a laugh that turns heads.  
**Personality:** Fiercely loyal and slow to trust.  
**Motivation:** Wants to atone for a mistake that cost others dearly.  
**Backstory:** Few in a mining settlement deep in the hills expected much of Vanya Fennick, until a fire destroyed everything he knew. Since then, Vanya has set out to atone for a mistake that cost others dearly.

**Background question:** What mentor taught you to control your untamed magic, and why are they no longer able to guide you?
