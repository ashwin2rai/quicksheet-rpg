## Features

### Ancestry: Human

**High Stamina.** Gain an additional Stress slot at character creation.

**Adaptability.** When you fail a roll that utilized one of your Experiences, you can **mark a Stress** to reroll.

### Community: Frostborne

**Hardy.** When you take a rest, you clear a Hit Point.

### Class: Brawler

**I Am the Weapon.** Your barehanded attacks are as strong as any blade. You have a primary weapon called Brawler's Strike equipped while you have no other Active Weapons. It uses a trait of your choice, has Melee range, and deals **d8+d6** physical damage using your Proficiency (both the d8 and d6 scale off your Proficiency). While this weapon is active, you gain a +1 bonus to your Evasion.

**Combo Strike.** *(Combo Die: d4)* After rolling damage on a successful attack with a Melee weapon, you can **mark a Stress** to start a combo strike. When you do, roll your Combo Die and note the result, then continue rolling your Combo Die until the result of your latest roll is lower than the roll that preceded it. You deal extra damage equal to the total of all rolled Combo Die results on this attack. The results can't be modified by any means.

Your Combo Die starts as a **d4**. Once per tier, you can increase your Combo Die by one step as a level advancement option.

**Square Up.** *(Hope feature)* **Spend 3 Hope** to intimidate a target within Close range, making them temporarily *Vulnerable*.

### Subclass: Martial Artist (Foundation)

**Stance Fighter.** *(Known stances are listed under Martial Stances; Focus: 6, the most you can hold)* You can channel your inner resolve to shift into martial stances that grant you special benefits in combat. Take the Martial Stances sheet and choose two martial stances from Tier 1. Each time you level up your character, choose an additional stance from your tier or lower.

### Martial Stances

Focus: once per rest during a moment of calm, you can clear your Focus track, then roll a number of d6s equal to your Instinct and gain Focus equal to the highest result rolled. You can hold a maximum of 6 Focus. You can spend a Focus to shift into a martial stance you know; you can't have more than one active stance at a time. You drop out of your active stance when you shift into a different stance, take Severe damage or mark your last Hit Point; otherwise, at the end of the scene.

- **Quick** (Tier 1): When you make an attack, you can spend a Focus or mark a Stress to target another creature within range with that attack.
- **Favored** (Tier 1): Gain a bonus to damage rolls equal to a trait of your choice.

### Forceful Push

*Valor 1 - Ability - Recall 0*

Make an attack with your primary weapon against a target within Melee range. On a success, you deal damage and knock them back to Close range. On a success with Hope, add a **d6** to your damage roll.

Additionally, you can **spend a Hope** to make them temporarily *Vulnerable*.

### I Am Your Shield

*Valor 1 - Ability - Recall 1*

When an ally within Very Close range would take damage, you can **mark a Stress** to stand in the way and make yourself the target of the attack instead. When you take damage from this attack, you can mark any number of Armor Slots.

## Character Hook

**High Concept:** Soft-Hearted Iron-Fisted Bouncer  
**Trouble:** Never Backs Down From a Dare
