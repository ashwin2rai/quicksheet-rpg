## Third-party game content

This product includes materials from the Daggerheart System Reference Document 2.0, © Critical Role, LLC. All rights reserved.

Darrington Press created the SRD material, and QuickSheet uses it subject to the [Darrington Press Community Gaming License](https://darringtonpress.com/license/).

**Modifications.** QuickSheet uses [Old Gus' Daggerheart System Reference Document](https://github.com/callmepartario/og-dhsrd) as its working reference. That is an edited and reorganized web edition of the SRD, prepared by Old Gus. QuickSheet further selects eligible game data from it, restructures it into JSON records with source tags, converts its text to Markdown, and formats it into generated character sheets. Where Old Gus differs from the official SRD 2.0, QuickSheet uses the official wording, and it adds a few records Old Gus does not include (the Martial Stances and Brawler's Strike) directly from the official SRD. The SRD-derived files are `data/core/*.json` (core game) and `data/hope-fear/*.json` (the Hope & Fear expansion material included in SRD 2.0). See `docs/data-provenance.md` for details.

The SRD's Combat Wheelchair rules, which QuickSheet includes in its equipment data, are credited in the SRD to **Mark Thompson**.
